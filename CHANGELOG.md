## [1.3.0](https://github.com/pusher/tweetnacl-swiftwrap/compare/1.2.0...1.3.0) - Unreleased

### Changed

- **Breaking:** Raised the minimum supported OS versions to iOS 15.0, macOS 12.0 and tvOS 15.0 (from iOS 13.0, macOS 10.15 and tvOS 13.0), required to build under Xcode 27. Apps still targeting older OS versions should pin to `1.2.0` or earlier.
